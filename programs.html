The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, taught on your own face and your own products.

Program 01 · Where everything shows

Skin prep & base

Base is where makeup goes wrong most visibly. We work on prep, matching shade and undertone in daylight rather than store lighting, applying thin layers, and setting only where you need it — which is rarely the whole face.

  • 10 weeks
  • Beginners
  • 20 min a day
  • What you already own

Program 02 · Ten minutes, repeatable

Everyday eyes

Hooded, downturned, deep set — eye shape changes where shadow and liner should sit, and most tutorials ignore it entirely. We work on your shape, on brows that frame rather than dominate, and on a look you can repeat half-asleep.

  • 10 weeks
  • Any level
  • 20 min a day
  • Two brushes

Start this program

Program 03 · Stop buying wrong

Color & undertones

Most people own several colors that fight their skin and one they wear every day. We work out your undertone, build a palette from it, and go through your existing collection honestly — keeping what works and retiring what doesn't.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • Your collection

Program 04 · It has to hold

Events & camera

Makeup that looks right in the mirror can look grey on camera or slide off by nine o'clock. We cover longwear technique, what flash does, what video lighting does, and a touch-up kit that fits in a small bag.

  • 8 weeks
  • Some experience
  • 2 hours a week
  • A mirror and a phone

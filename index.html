How we work

Coaching, not a counter

Soullviera sells no cosmetics and takes no commission from any brand. That matters in an industry where nearly every recommendation is paid for somewhere along the line.

We also stay out of skincare. Acne, rosacea, reactions and anything else your skin is doing belong with a physician or dermatologist, and every new product deserves a patch test before it goes near your face.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I need to buy new products?

No. We start with what you own, and we earn nothing from any brand. Often the issue is technique or shade, not the product.

I'm completely new to makeup.

That's the easiest place to start, honestly — no habits to undo. The base and eyes programs assume nothing.

Can you help with my skin?

No. We coach application only. Skin conditions go to a dermatologist, and we'll say so rather than guess.

My eyes are hooded and nothing works.

Eye shape is the single most ignored factor in tutorials. Placement changes completely, and that's most of the everyday eyes program.

Is this for professional makeup artistry?

It's for doing your own makeup well. If you want to work professionally, the technique transfers but licensing rules vary by state and we don't cover that.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

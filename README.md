## Business description (Stripe, directories…)

```
Coaching in makeup application, delivered online: one-on-one video sessions and small group classes covering skin prep and base, everyday eye technique, color matching and undertones, and looks for events and on camera. Clients follow an 8 to 12 week program with video feedback on the looks they practice at home. This is coaching on technique only; we sell no cosmetics and give no skincare or medical advice. Services are sold as month-to-month subscriptions from $29 to $159 per month, cancellable at any time. No physical products are sold or shipped. Site: soullviera.shop
```
